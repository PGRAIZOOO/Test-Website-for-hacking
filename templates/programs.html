Educational Programs & Curriculum

A sequential journey from sensory-based discovery to advanced academic inquiry.

Level 01

The Montessori Kindergarten (Ages 3-6)

Nurturing the "Absorbent Mind" through the Prepared Environment.

Foundational Pillars

Our Kindergarten program follows the authentic Montessori method, where children are grouped in multi-age settings to foster peer-to-peer learning and social harmony.

Practical Life

Activities like pouring, lacing, and grace/courtesy that build concentration and fine motor control.

Sensorial

Refining the senses through the Pink Tower, Brown Stairs, and Color Tablets to understand dimension and order.

Mathematics

Introduction to the Decimal System using Golden Beads, Number Rods, and the Spindle Box.

Language Arts

Phonetic awareness via Sandpaper Letters and the Moveable Alphabet to jumpstart early reading.

Level 02

Junior High School (Grades 7-10)

Fostering independence, critical thinking, and civic responsibility.

Advanced Academic Specialization

As students transition into young adulthood, our JHS curriculum bridges the gap between creative freedom and the rigorous standards required for Senior High and College entry.

  • STEM Integration: Hands-on Science labs focusing on Biology and Physics, paired with Robotics workshops.
  • The Trust Protocol: A signature seminar series introducing Blockchain basics and Digital Ethics.
  • Communication & Humanities: Deep dives into World Literature, Philosophy, and Public Speaking modules.
  • Livelihood & Tech: Practical skills in UI/UX design, introductory coding (Lua/Python), and financial literacy.

Elective Clubs

Students can join the Robotics Guild, Vocal Ensemble, or the Student Council for leadership development.
