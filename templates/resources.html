Enrollment Pathway

Follow these four steps to join the Huckleberry community.

Step 1: Inquiry & School Tour

Contact the registrar to schedule a morning tour of our Montessori facilities.

Step 2: Diagnostic Assessment

The student undergoes a readiness evaluation and an interview with the level coordinator.

Step 3: Document Submission

Submit PSA Birth Certificate, Form 137, and a Good Moral Certificate from the previous school.

Step 4: Final Registration

Settle tuition fees and receive the student ID and uniform claim stubs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer sibling discounts?

Yes, we offer a 10% discount on the tuition of the second child and 15% for the third child.

Is the uniform mandatory?

Yes, the prescribed uniform must be worn Monday through Thursday. Fridays are "Org Shirt" days.

What is the teacher-to-student ratio?

To ensure personalized attention, our Kinder ratio is 15:1 and JHS is 25:1.
