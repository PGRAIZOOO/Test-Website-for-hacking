Student Services

Comprehensive support systems designed to nurture student growth, safety, and academic success.

Our Holistic Support

At Huckleberry Montessori, we care about more than just grades. Our student services are integrated into the daily life of our learners to ensure they feel safe, healthy, and prepared for the future.

Core Student Services

Guidance & Wellness

Professional guidance to support students' mental health, emotional intelligence, and social development.

  • One-on-One Counseling
  • Social-Emotional Learning (SEL)
  • Peer Mediation Programs
  • Behavioral Support Plans

Health & Clinic Services

A fully-equipped school clinic with registered medical professionals on standby during school hours.

  • Daily Health Screenings
  • Emergency First Aid Response
  • Regular Dental & Physical Exams
  • Health & Nutrition Education

Academic Resource Help

Dedicated staff available to help students navigate research tools and foster a love for reading.

  • Research & Citation Support
  • Digital Resource Navigation
  • Literacy Tutoring Sessions
  • Curated Reading Lists

Campus Store

Convenient access to all necessary school supplies, official uniforms, and learning modules.

  • Official School Uniforms
  • Montessori Learning Materials
  • Basic Stationery & Art Supplies
  • Huckleberry Merch & Apparel

Safe-Track Transport

Reliable and monitored school transportation services for a stress-free commute for parents and students.

  • Door-to-Door Pickup/Drop-off
  • Real-time GPS Shuttle Tracking
  • Trained & Background-Checked Drivers
  • Air-Conditioned Comfort

Future Pathways

Strategic programs that help older students prepare for the transition to higher education and future careers.

  • College Entrance Exam Prep
  • Career Aptitude Assessments
  • Internship & Workshop Access
  • Resume & Interview Coaching

Huckleberry Montessori

Nurturing curiosity and independence through the Montessori method. Empowering students to become lifelong learners.
