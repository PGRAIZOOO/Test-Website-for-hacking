Our Story

Founded in 1999, Huckleberry Montessori School began as a small preschool with a single mission: to provide an authentic Montessori experience that respects the individuality of the child. What started as a modest classroom has grown into a premier K-10 institution.

We believe that education is not the filling of a pail, but the lighting of a fire. For over 25 years, we have integrated the traditional Maria Montessori method with modern technological literacy, ensuring our students are prepared for both life and the future digital economy.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a carefully prepared environment that fosters self-discipline, curiosity, and a sense of social responsibility. We empower students to master their own learning journey through hands-on discovery.

👁️

Our Vision

To be the leading Montessori school in the Philippines, recognized for producing graduates who are not only academically excellent but also empathetic, innovative, and ready to lead in a decentralized world.

💎
